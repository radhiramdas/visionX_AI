About The System

Intelligent Eye Screening with AI

VisionX AI is an AI-assisted eye screening prototype that analyzes retinal fundus images to classify seven eye-related categories. It combines deep learning with visual explanations to help users understand model predictions.

Pioneering Retinal Disease Detection

Retinopathy and macular degeneration often develop asymptomatically in early stages. VisionX AI leverages fine-tuned convolutional neural network architectures to detect subtle pathological lesions, vascular changes, and optic disc anomalies in high-resolution fundus photography.

Supported Screening Categories:

AMD Cataract Diabetic Retinopathy (DR) Glaucoma Hypertensive Retinopathy Normal Fundus Pathological Myopia

Medical Notice: VisionX AI is an academic clinical screening-support tool and does not replace a professional eye examination or ophthalmological diagnosis.

Core Capabilities

Designed for Clinical Clarity & Precision

Ophthalmology Conditions

Seven Supported Retinal Categories

Educational overview of the pathological conditions recognized by the VisionX AI deep learning classification pipeline.

Macula

Age-Related Macular Degeneration (AMD)

Degeneration of the central retina (macula) causing blurred or diminished central vision, characterized by drusen deposits and retinal pigmentary changes.

Condition 01 Class: AMD
Lens Opacity

Cataract

Clouding of the internal crystalline lens resulting in obscured fundus illumination, diffuse blur, and impaired retinal visibility during screening.

Condition 02 Class: Cataract
Vascular

Diabetic Retinopathy (DR)

Microvascular complication of diabetes manifesting through microaneurysms, hemorrhages, hard exudates, and abnormal neovascular proliferation.

Condition 03 Class: DR
Optic Nerve

Glaucoma

Progressive optic neuropathy characterized by enlarged optic cup-to-disc ratio (CDR), neuroretinal rim thinning, and gradual peripheral field loss.

Condition 04 Class: Glaucoma
Systemic / Vessels

Hypertensive Retinopathy

Retinal vascular damage induced by sustained high systemic blood pressure, featuring arteriolar narrowing, copper/silver wiring, and AV nicking.

Condition 05 Class: Hypertensive_Retinopathy
Healthy Baseline

Normal Fundus

Healthy baseline retina demonstrating sharp optic disc margins, distinct physiological cup, intact foveal reflex, and clear vascular architecture.

Condition 06 Class: Normal_Fundus
Chorioretinal

Pathological Myopia

High myopic refractive error with excessive globe elongation leading to posterior staphyloma, peripapillary atrophy, and chorioretinal thinning.

Condition 07 Class: Pathological_Myopia

Register Patient
